## 3. Candidate experience (step by step)

### Step 1 — Shortlist notification (template message)

The candidate receives an approved **Utility** WhatsApp template:

> **Hi {First name}, congratulations! You have been shortlisted for the Lead Business Analyst position at Masai School. Our team will share next steps with you shortly. Reply here if you have any questions.**

This opens the conversation. **No questions are sent in this first message.**

### Step 2 — Candidate replies (anything)

When the candidate sends **any reply** (e.g. “Hi”, “Thanks”, “Yes”), TigerHire sends an intro plus **Question 1 of 7**:

> Great news — you've been shortlisted for Lead Business Analyst at Masai School. The hiring team has 7 quick questions before the next step. Please reply YES or NO to each.
>
> **(1/7)** {first question}
>
> Reply YES or NO.

If they reply **“No”** explicitly to the invite, the flow closes politely and their profile stays on file.

### Step 3 — One question at a time

- Each **YES** or **NO** is recorded against that specific question.
- The next question is sent automatically.
- **All 7 must be answered** to complete the flow.
- After the last answer: _“Thanks — that's all the questions for Lead Business Analyst. The hiring team has your answers and will be in touch with next steps.”_

### Step 4 — What we do _not_ do automatically

- We **do not** auto-reject on a “No” during post-shortlist screening.
- FitScore is **not** re-run or blocked by these answers.
- Ishita **reviews** answers in TigerHire and decides next steps.
