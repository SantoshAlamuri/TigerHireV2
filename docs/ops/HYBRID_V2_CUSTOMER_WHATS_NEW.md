# Hybrid v2 — Customer "What's New" Draft

Subject: Better-fit verdict cards are now live

Body:

We have upgraded TigerHire verdict cards for newer FitScore runs. You will now see:

- short evidence quotes from the candidate CV supporting the verdict,
- a confidence band (high/medium/low) alongside the score,
- clearer verdict actions for faster shortlist decisions.

This upgrade does not change your hiring workflow steps. It improves explainability so your team can review and defend decisions faster.

If any verdict looks unclear, share the candidate and role with support and we will review it with you.
